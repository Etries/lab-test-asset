How this works

The app edits its own data and keeps the Excel workbook in step with it. Think of the app as the editor and the workbook as an always-current copy for reading and sharing. When you (or a colleague) edit the workbook by hand, bring it back in with Import above — that makes the uploaded file the new truth. Avoid editing the workbook and the app at the very same time, since the last import wins.
